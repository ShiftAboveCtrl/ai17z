`v1.0.0-beta.83` lets you write an agent's trading mandate, and protect a real position while you are asleep.

## A trading mandate you write

An agent's Wallet section now has a **Trading mandate** editor, in SOL and
percent rather than base units: how much a trade may spend, a day, in total,
and how many positions at once. Every buy still waits for your approval. A
memecoin agent may be allowed **any Solana token** within those limits, because
a token launched an hour ago cannot be listed in advance. Pause stops it at
once.

## Protective exits

The same editor can authorise sales in advance, and only sales: a stop loss, a
trailing stop once a position is in profit, and a liquidity floor, each with the
worst slippage, price impact and fee such a sale may accept. A guardian on this
machine checks every real position every fifteen seconds, without asking a
model, and when one crosses it prepares the sale, checks it against your bounds,
and sends it. It can never buy, never raise a limit, and nothing an agent
decides can write or widen it. Off unless you set it.

## Restore a wallet from its recovery phrase

A Solana wallet can be restored from the twelve or twenty-four words you hold,
using the same derivation as Phantom and Solflare. You say which address you
expect, and a phrase that derives a different one is refused. The phrase is
used once and stored nowhere.

## Upgrading

Three migrations, applied on start. Existing mandates keep their behaviour: no
protective exits, and only the assets they list.

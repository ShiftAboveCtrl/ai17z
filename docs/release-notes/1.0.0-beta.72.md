`v1.0.0-beta.72` lets an owner convene a council over a question, reads sets
of public posts somebody supplies, and puts the inbox in an order worth
reading.

## A council

`POST /api/agents/:id/council` asks up to five specialists (research, market,
onchain, a skeptic and risk) to judge one proposition from the same evidence.
Each member is one call through the owner's own configured model, with a
deadline, and sees only the evidence given, fenced as somebody else's writing.
A member whose answer cites none of that evidence is dropped and the report
says so, rather than counting an opinion with nothing behind it. The verdict
is assembled by fixed rules, not by another model call, so the same views
always produce the same consensus. The owner's agent only; it spends the
owner's own model budget.

## Reading posts somebody supplies

Four reference capabilities read a set of public posts the caller hands over
and fetch nothing themselves:

- `social.coordination_signals`: the same wording from many authors,
  posting in the same minute, amplification concentrated in a few accounts.
  Each is a signal with a strength and a caveat, never a claim about a person,
  and ordinary conversation produces none.
- `social.narrative_genesis`: the earliest post in the set that carries a
  term, how its wording changed and how it spread. Earliest in the set, said
  as exactly that.
- `social.relationship_graph`: who replied to, mentioned, quoted and reposted
  whom, with counts and first and last times.
- `social.public_profile`: what an author writes about and how, measured from
  their posts in the set, and nothing about the person.

They sit in the Reference & Knowledge pack and on the shared runtime.

## The inbox, in an order worth reading

Every inbox row now carries a triage: a priority, a suggestion (answer, needs
you, can be left, nothing to do) and the named factors behind it. A reply held
for approval or one that failed comes first; somebody who wrote to the agent
and asked something ranks above something the radar found; age counts
against. Spam is a reason to leave something, never to delete it, and how
many people follow somebody plays no part. It suggests and never acts: the
inbox keeps arrival order unless "most worth a look first" is chosen, and
answering stays with the pipeline and its approvals.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. No migration, no setting.

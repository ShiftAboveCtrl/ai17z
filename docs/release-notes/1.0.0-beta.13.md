AI17Z is a local-first platform for running autonomous agents on your own machine. An agent is an identity, a memory, a model, a policy, and a set of channels it can act on.

## A like that had already happened was reported as a failure

If your agent liked or reposted the same post a second time, the second one was recorded as having failed. The post was liked. Your agent was told it was not.

Liking is a desired state rather than a switch: the agent opens the post, sees the like is already there, and clicks nothing. That part was right and stayed right. What was wrong was the bookkeeping afterwards, which used a check meant for something else entirely — the one that stops the same words being sent twice to the same person. A like has no words, so the check was reading the post instead, and it turned "do not send this sentence twice" into "this account may never make sure that post is liked again".

The visible cost was an agent that believed a thing it had just done had failed, and said so.

## Your agent is now actually reading its own account

The Analytics screen was given a follower history in the last release. On any account that posts regularly, it was never going to fill in.

The reading was set to happen when your agent checked its recent posts and found none worth checking — which sounded reasonable and had it exactly backwards. An account that posts often always has something recent to check, so the accounts most worth measuring were the ones that would never have been measured.

It now happens on its own schedule, about four times a day, on the visit your agent was already making. Nothing extra is asked of your account, and nothing was lost by the change: the post check simply happens on the next visit a minute later.

If your history is still empty, it fills in from the first reading after you update. Two readings make a series, and until there are two the screen says so rather than drawing a line through one point.

## Everything from Beta 1.0.0 (12)

**Liking and reposting can be switched on.** Before Beta 1.0.0 (12) the screen said Allowed and saved nothing. If you switched either on before that release, switch it on again — there was nothing there to carry forward.

**Twelve things your agent can reach for on X while it is answering** — posts, profiles, conversations, search, notifications, follower lists, timelines, your direct message inbox, a post's own figures, and liking and reposting. Reading is allowed by default; anything that changes something is off until you turn it on; the two message surfaces ask you each time.

**X Studio** — a screen of its own showing what your agent has seen and what came of it: what might be worth answering and everything it passed over, what several accounts have started saying, who leads somewhere new, what has actually worked, what is being launched, the idea backlog, and one experiment at a time.

**Experiments say "not yet" for a fortnight**, and there is no setting to change that.

**Posts measure themselves**, by reading the counts that were already on a page your agent had already opened.

## Updating

Nothing updates itself. AI17Z asks GitHub what has been released at most once every six hours, shows you the notes, and offers a link. You can ignore it, skip a version permanently, or turn the check off entirely.

Run this installer over the top of an earlier one. Your agents, memories, provider keys and settings live in your data folder and are not touched. The first start after an upgrade rebuilds and says so.

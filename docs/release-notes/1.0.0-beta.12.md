AI17Z is a local-first platform for running autonomous agents on your own machine. An agent is an identity, a memory, a model, a policy, and a set of channels it can act on.

## Liking and reposting could not be switched on

If you turned on "Like a post on X" or "Repost on X" for an agent, the screen said Allowed and nothing was saved. The agent went on refusing, quietly, for as long as you left it.

The switch was being written into the built-in tool catalogue, and capabilities have never been in that catalogue — so the write matched no row, saved nothing, and reported success. Reading capabilities are allowed by default, so all of those worked and the fault stayed hidden. Writing capabilities are off by default, which meant they could not be turned on at all.

Your decisions about capabilities now have their own place to live, and turning one on stays on. If you had switched either of these on before, switch it on again: there was nothing there to carry forward.

Nothing was ever done without your permission by this — the failure was in the direction of refusing, not acting.

## Your agent watches its own account now

The Analytics screen has always been able to show how your following has moved over time. It had nothing to show, because nothing in AI17Z ever opened your own profile to look.

It does now, and it costs nothing extra: your agent already checks its recent posts every few minutes for replies that never produced a notification, and when it has no recent post to check, that visit reads your account instead. No new schedule, no extra load on your account, and no request that was not already being made.

Two readings make a series. Until there are two, the screen says so rather than drawing a line through one point.

## Everything from Beta 1.0.0 (11)

**Your agent can look things up on X while it is answering** — twelve capabilities covering posts, profiles, conversations, search, notifications, follower lists, timelines, your direct message inbox, a post's own figures, and liking and reposting. Reading is allowed by default; anything that changes something is off until you turn it on; the two message surfaces ask each time.

**X Studio** — a screen of its own showing what your agent has seen and what came of it: what might be worth answering and everything it passed over, what several accounts have started saying, who leads somewhere new, what has actually worked, what is being launched, the idea backlog, and one experiment at a time.

**Experiments say "not yet" for a fortnight**, and there is no setting to change that.

**Posts measure themselves**, by reading the counts that were already on a page your agent had already opened.

## Updating

Nothing updates itself. AI17Z asks GitHub what has been released at most once every six hours, shows you the notes, and offers a link. You can ignore it, skip a version permanently, or turn the check off entirely.

Run this installer over the top of an earlier one. Your agents, memories, provider keys and settings live in your data folder and are not touched. The first start after an upgrade rebuilds and says so.

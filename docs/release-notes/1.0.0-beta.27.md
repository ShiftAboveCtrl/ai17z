**Two things found by running Beta 3.5 on a real installation rather than by reading it.** Everything in Beta 3.5 and Beta 3.6 is here too.

## Trying your agent against a post does not put it in your inbox

The Lab runs the real pipeline, which is the whole reason its answer is worth anything: it manufactures the same kind of event a mention arrives on, so the agent gets the same context, memory, research and checks a real reply gets.

It also meant every trial landed in your inbox looking like somebody had written to you, and a trial held for review was counted as waiting for you to decide, when nothing had been sent and nothing could be. Measured on the test installation the moment the first real-post rehearsal finished: the badge went from one to two.

The same trial would have shown in your mentions as a conversation, and its draft as an answer that went out.

Both lists now skip rehearsals. Nothing else changes about them.

## The Lab says several things as several things

A stage usually has more than one finding behind it, and they were being run together into one sentence that said none of them:

> Passed the filter. Passed 100% of the phrasing appeared in a recent reply

That is three findings, and the last one is the reason the reply was held. They are separated now, and a finding recorded twice is shown once: the check before publication and the check at the door both say "Passed".

## Also in this release

Beta 3.6's two Telegram fixes, for anyone who has a bot connected: a command typed hours ago is no longer treated as an instruction when the bot first reads its backlog, and `/start`, which is the button Telegram puts on every bot, no longer lifts a pause. The settings screen also says who else can drive your bot, because the chat is what identifies you and a group means everybody in it.

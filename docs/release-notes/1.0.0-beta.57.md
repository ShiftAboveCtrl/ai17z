**An agent's own posts now have to be worth reading, and spam no longer gets its attention.** This release came from reading what a live agent actually posted, tracing each weak post back to where it started, and fixing those paths rather than the prompt.

## Posting

- **No more echoes of its own replies.** After a reply, the agent used to queue "say more about" that subject as a post, so it kept re-posting what it had just told one person. That is gone.
- **Only its own conclusions become posts.** Something it merely saw on X, including somebody else's post about it, is no longer turned into its "own" idea.
- **A quality check before every original post.** A draft has to have a point, must not repeat a recent post, must not be another announcement about the product when the last few were, and must not fish for engagement. A draft that fails is not posted, the reason is recorded, and the idea is set aside rather than drafted again. Silence is a valid outcome.
- **Beliefs learned from what it says are cleaner.** A reply no longer teaches it a "position" on the person it answered or on itself, and a position keeps the sentence that states it rather than the whole reply. Positions learned under the old rules can be reviewed and retired with a record of why; nothing you pinned is touched.
- **It knows a few true things about itself.** Asked what it is up to or what it has learned, it is given its release, what it published this week and what its learning is doing, and nothing private.
- **Findings are used, not narrated.** When it looks something up, it talks about what it found rather than about the lookup.
- **Test this agent** now includes sixteen everyday situations: jokes, light trolling, "wen moon", engagement bait, a meme, nonsense, a compliment, disagreement, news that is and is not its subject.

## Spam

- **Spam is judged before it costs anything.** Posts with no words, links with a pitch, mention stuffing, and the same text from many accounts or many times from one are filtered on arrival: no thread is read, no model is asked, and no reply slot is used.
- **No account or word is spam by being present.** A post that tags @grok is judged on what it says. Marking one spammy post as spam applies to that post only; a later real question that tags the same accounts is answered as usual.
- **You decide.** Every post has Mark as spam or Not spam, and an author can be muted from the agent's attention. Not spam is a strong correction: the post is offered to the agent straight away and that text is trusted from then on.
- **Campaigns are one thing, not fifty.** Many accounts pasting one text are grouped, with how many and who kept for review.
- **See what it did.** Activity shows what was filtered this week and roughly how many model calls that saved. Filtered posts stay under "Filtered as spam".
- **Learning is spam-adjusted.** Spam replies no longer count as engagement, and distinct people who actually replied count for more than likes.

**AI17Z can now link to AI17Z Studio, use the Plugins you have there, and finish a purchase with your own wallet, and nothing about your agents ever leaves your machine to do it.** Linking is optional: an installation that never links works exactly as before.

## Linking to Studio

A new Studio tab on the Plugins screen links this installation to your Studio account with an eight-letter code, the way a TV links to a streaming account.

- **Your installation always starts the conversation.** Studio never connects to it and has no address to connect to.
- **What is sent:** a public key, a name you can change ("AI17Z on Windows"), the platform and the AI17Z version. **What is never sent:** your agents, memories, prompts, conversations, provider keys, X sessions, Chrome profiles, files or the master key.
- **The installation's identity is a key made on your machine.** The private half is sealed under your master key and never leaves; every request is signed with it, so a token copied off your machine is useless anywhere else. The code is only for pairing.
- **Disconnect** tells Studio first and frees this installation's seats. **Relinking after a reset** suggests the installation you had before, and you confirm on Studio that its seats should come across. Purchases belong to your account and never move.
- **Revoked on the website**, the installation learns it on its next check and marketplace Plugins stop at once.

## Plugins from Studio

- **What you may use arrives signed by Studio**, bound to this installation and valid for 72 hours. A Plugin from the marketplace runs only while that signature covers exactly the version installed. Editing the local database cannot unlock one.
- **Offline is fine.** If Studio cannot be reached, marketplace Plugins keep working until the last check runs out, and after that only they pause, and say why. Nothing else in AI17Z is affected.
- **Hosted Plugins** run through Studio's gateway, which checks your entitlement on every call. Paid Plugins are always hosted.
- **Entitlements sync every quarter of an hour**, from the loop that already runs, and on demand from the Studio tab.

## Buying a Plugin

You start a purchase on Studio and choose which of your linked installations will finish it. That AI17Z shows the purchase, checks every term against what it already knows, and asks **your own wallet** in your browser to sign one thing: a transfer of the exact $AI17Z price to the publisher, on Robinhood Chain.

- **Only that transaction can be prepared.** Another chain, token, amount format, or recipient is refused whatever Studio says. There is no approve, no swap and no other call.
- **Never twice.** Once your wallet has been asked, AI17Z will not ask again until you say nothing was sent or the transaction is recorded. A record of every purchase stays on your machine.
- **Your agents cannot pay for anything.** Paying is not a capability: no model can reach it, and AI17Z never holds a wallet key.
- Studio grants the Plugin only after reading the transfer on the chain itself, at finality.

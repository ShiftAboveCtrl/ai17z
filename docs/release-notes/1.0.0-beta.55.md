**Plugins on AI17Z Studio can now be bought once, monthly or yearly, in ETH or AI17Z, and AI17Z pays for them the same careful way: one exact transfer at a time, from your own wallet, only when you press the button.** Nothing changes for an installation that never links to Studio.

## Buying and renewing

- **Plans.** A Plugin on Studio is free, a one-time purchase, or a monthly or yearly subscription. Paid plans are priced in ETH.
- **Two currencies.** Pay in ETH, or in AI17Z at a live quoted price with a discount for paying in AI17Z (4% today). The quote is the median of recent prices read from the AI17Z/ETH pool on chain and checked against an independent source; when it cannot be trusted, paying in AI17Z is switched off and ETH still works.
- **A marketplace fee, paid openly.** The fee (8% today) is a separate transfer to the marketplace treasury, shown before you sign. The AI17Z discount comes out of the fee, so the publisher receives the same value whichever currency you pick.
- **One transfer per payment, each checked twice.** A checkout is the publisher's share and, on a paid plan, the fee. Each is exactly a plain ETH transfer with no data, or a transfer of $AI17Z, to the recipient Studio recorded when the checkout began. Anything else Studio asks for is refused, and the Plugins screen checks the transaction again before your wallet sees it. There is still no approve, no swap and no other call.
- **Half paid is never paid twice.** If one payment goes through and the other does not, AI17Z asks only for the one still owed, and the purchase completes when it lands. A payment the chain refused can be sent again, and the refused one stays on record.
- **Subscriptions never charge you.** Nothing renews by itself and no allowance is ever requested. Renew early and the new period follows the old one, so no paid time is lost; renew after it ends and the period starts when your payment confirms.
- **When a subscription ends,** the Plugin stays installed and visible and stops working until you renew. Studio's gateway refuses it on the very next call, whatever this machine's clock says, and the signed permission AI17Z holds never outlasts what was paid for.

## On the Plugins screen

- **Studio tab:** each Plugin you may use shows its plan, when it is paid through, and **Renew** once it has ended. A newly bought Plugin shows **Install**.
- **Pending purchases** list every payment of a checkout on its own: who it goes to, how much, in what, and whether it has landed, with the quote and the fee spelled out.
- **A Plugin you buy starts switched off for every agent.** Buying and installing it are not a decision about which agents may use it; choose them under Installed. Other Plugins keep their existing defaults.

## Wallets

- **Every wallet in your browser is offered by name**, found through EIP-6963 first, then Phantom's own provider, then the legacy one, and late-announcing wallets are asked again. Pick Phantom, Backpack or MetaMask and that wallet opens.
- **Link a wallet to your Studio account from inside AI17Z**, and review a purchase against the chain before your wallet is asked.

## Also

- **Stopping AI17Z forgets the native browser worker it stopped**, so starting again straight away starts a new one rather than waiting on a record of the old one.

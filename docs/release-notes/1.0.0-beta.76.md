`v1.0.0-beta.76` gives agents and AI17Z Studio's trading terminal what a
memecoin trader looks at first, and makes paper trades pay for the size they
trade at.

## Reading a market the way a trader does

Five new read-only capabilities, offered to agents that are allowed to look
things up and to the shared runtime Studio uses:

- **`market.search`** finds every token a name, symbol or address could mean,
  each with its chain and exact address, deepest pool first, and says when
  several share a symbol. It never chooses one.
- **`market.pair_detail`** reads one exact pair: price, liquidity, market cap,
  FDV, buys, sells, volume and price change over five minutes, an hour, six
  hours and a day, when it was created, and the token's own links. Only `http`
  and `https` links survive.
- **`market.recent_trades`** reads the latest swaps through one exact pool.
- **`token.solana_profile`** reads what Jupiter's token index knows about one
  Solana mint: holders and how concentrated they are, how much the developer
  holds and how many tokens they launched, mint and freeze authority, the
  token program, organic activity and verification.
- **`market.discover_solana`** lists new, trending, most traded and most
  organically active Solana tokens with the same facts.

A number a source did not report is reported as missing, never as zero.
Jupiter is read without a key, at well under its published keyless limit; a
`JUPITER_API_KEY` raises the ceiling when one is set.

## Paper trades pay for their size

A paper fill used to happen at the mid price, as though every pool were
infinitely deep. Against a pool that reports its depth it now receives what a
trade of that size would, modelled as constant product on the reported depth,
which overstates the cost on a concentrated pool rather than flattering the
fill. The fill records the impact and how it was worked out, and a trade that
would move the price further than it allows is refused. Bonding curves and
brokers are not modelled this way, and say so.

## Memecoin pools can be priced

GeckoTerminal reports no fee for pump.fun or PumpSwap pools, so the pool
reader, which will not quote a pool that does not say what it charges, could
price almost no new Solana token. Those two now use the ceiling of pump.fun's
published fee schedule (1.25%, as of 2026-10-08), named in the snapshot's
source. Every other pool with no stated fee is still not quoted.

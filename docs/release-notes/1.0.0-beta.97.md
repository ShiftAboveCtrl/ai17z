Beta 0.97 teaches the token evidence card to answer more kinds of question, from screenshots to single transactions, and adds a set of opt-in Studio features.

## What changed since Beta 0.96

**Token checks read more, and say what they could not read.** Every answer below keeps the card's rules: each reading carries its source and time, a reading that is missing says so and is never shown as zero, and no reply names a winner, calls anything safe or unsafe, or gives a verdict.

- **Check a token from a screenshot.** A "check this" mention with no address in its text sends the attached image, or the image on the post above, to the `vision` role. Only a full Solana or EVM address is accepted. The reply opens with `read from your image: <address>` so a misread is visible. It refuses with a sentence when the image holds two addresses, when an address is shortened or unclear, or when a shortened copy disagrees with the full one. An address typed in the text always wins and the image is not read.
- **Why is it moving.** A message such as "why is <mint> moving" gets then, now and change for price, volume and liquidity over 5 minutes, 1 hour (the default), 6 hours or 24 hours, each with its source, read time and pool depth. A missing earlier reading says so. The reply always ends "caused by: unknown, readings only". It reads market sources only, never X.
- **Explain this transaction.** One Solana signature, bare or inside an explorer link, gets a fixed reading from the node: status, slot, block time, fee, the programs called, each instruction's decoded type and each account's SOL and token balances before and after. Anything not recorded prints "not recorded" and a section the node did not send prints "not read". It never guesses intent, never says who owns an address and never talks about profit or loss. Two signatures, a string that is not a signature, or a "whose is this" question get a one-sentence refusal and no read.
- **The deployer.** The card names the account that paid to create the mint, but only when the chain proves it: the mint's oldest signature must have succeeded and carry `initializeMint` for that exact mint. Otherwise the deployer is reported as unavailable. A launch count appears only when the index agrees with the chain.
- **LP burn and lock.** For Solana pools the card adds how much of the LP supply is burned and how much sits in a Streamflow lock that has not ended, with the unlock time. A missing pool or unreadable account prints `LP status: unavailable`, never 0% or "not locked". A holder that is a program or escrow account is reported as held by a contract and is not counted as locked. Streamflow escrow accounts cannot be traced to their stream within the allowed reads, so those locks read as held by a contract for now.
- **Holder map.** With `tools.holderMap` on, a "holder map" ask about a Solana token gets a picture of its top holders, grouping accounts first funded by the same address. It reads at most 12 holders. Program-owned accounts and program funders are never grouped. The funding address never leaves the installation, and the picture carries an opaque group id only. The wording is fixed: a reading, not proof of one owner, not a verdict. It falls back to text when Studio is unlinked or slow.
- **Check A vs B and the card as a picture.** Two addresses on one chain answer with both cards side by side, in the order asked and with no ranking. `tools.evidenceCardImage` adds an optional picture of the card from the linked Studio, and any failure sends the text card unchanged.
- **Follow up when a confirmed watch crosses.** With `tools.evidenceFollowUps` on, the agent can reply once under the post that asked, with the before, at-card and current values. The text is fixed, no model writes it, and each crossing is answered at most once.

The transaction explainer and the "why is it moving" reply use the existing evidence card switch, `tools.evidenceCards`, which is off by default. Both keep its asker limits, cooldowns and research budget, and both check the switch and permission again before sending.

**Two new posts built from what the agent already read.** Both are off by default and advanced only.

- **Narrative pulse.** A daily post that sets what the agent's own reading is talking about beside what people checked on AI17Z Studio. It is built only from posts already read, so it adds no read of X. Subjects are ordered by share, each with its sample size. "Rising" needs a measured snapshot 12 to 48 hours old and a 1.5 times move. Subjects the agent stays quiet about, names of accounts or people, and subjects with coordination signals are dropped. The post says it does not endorse anything and says when the checks half could not be read. Switches: `studio.postNarrativePulse` and `studio.contributeNarrativeTerms`.
- **Weekly receipts recap.** A weekly post listing every signal the radar published in the last seven days with what each stated reading measured 24 hours later. Counts and readings only, small samples say "n = 4", and there is no win rate, profit or ranking. Only signals that were really published count. Switches: `studio.postReceiptsRecap` and an optional picture, `studio.receiptsRecapImage`. Any picture failure posts as text.

**Studio features.**

- Coin alerts from Studio can become the radar's universe, per agent, with an owner switch under the risk watches panel. Only token addresses are imported, at most 25, one per poll inside the usual read budget. They are noticed, never answered on their own.
- The "most checked today" board has two opt-in settings, `studio.contributeChecks` and `studio.postMostChecked`. Only the mint, the hour and the number of distinct askers are sent, and only for checks made after the owner opted in.
- An owner can publish the agent's paper setup to Studio as a holder playbook, after previewing the exact document.
- A paper record post can end with the link to its proven page on the linked Studio (`studio.linkPaperRecord`).
- An agent can state facts about the $AI17Z token from Studio's published document (`ai17z_token.facts`, off by default).
- Activity tape receipts record which network a reply went out on.

**Telegram has two new read-only commands.** `radar` shows what the radar is watching and what it found most recently. `standing` shows each agent's latest league entry and fill counts, or says there is none. Farcaster reads now have a budget of their own, 60 reads per 10 minutes by default, so they no longer draw on X's.

**A receipt claim can no longer deadlock a finishing read.** The weekly claim and a read finishing the same receipt took database locks in opposite orders, and CI caught one deadlock. The two-window guarantee was never broken. The claim now takes a lock that still keeps claimers apart but no longer blocks the other side's foreign key check.

**Update check.** `update-ai17z -Check` now reads its release source in the right order.

This adds migrations 0138 to 0142.

## Known limits

Several of these features need Studio routes that ship separately, and until they are live the feature reports Studio as unavailable or posts text alone: the evidence card picture, the holder map picture, the receipts recap picture, coin alerts, the check board and the narrative pulse checks half. Everything here has been exercised against mocks and test databases. No run used a live Solana node, a live vision model reading a real screenshot, or live X. The transaction reader does not yet cover address lookup tables or Token-2022 fields.

## Upgrading

Use the updater belonging to the installation you want to update. Beta 0.97 uses the semver tag `v1.0.0-beta.97`. GitHub Actions builds the Windows, Ubuntu and macOS packages and verifies the published release files.

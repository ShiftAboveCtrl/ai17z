AI17Z is a local-first platform for running autonomous agents on your own machine. An agent is an identity, a memory, a model, a policy, and a set of channels it can act on.

## Your agent can now look things up on X while it is answering

Until now an agent read exactly what the pipeline handed it. If somebody replied "what did he mean by that" about a post further up a thread, the agent had the mention and nothing else.

It can now reach for twelve things on X mid-answer, and it chooses which:

**Reading** — one post, a profile, a whole conversation, a search, your notifications, who follows an account, a timeline (home, following, bookmarks, a list, a community), your direct message inbox, one conversation in it, and the figures X shows only the author of a post.

**Acting** — liking and reposting.

Every one of them is a switch you hold. Reading is allowed by default because an agent that looks something up unasked is useful. Anything that changes something on X is **off** until you turn it on, and the two message surfaces ask you each time — a timeline is public, and an inbox is correspondence between two people only one of whom is you.

**There is no capability that sends a direct message.** A reply on a timeline is public and answerable in the open; a message is not. That is a decision, not an omission, and there is a test that keeps it one.

You can see all of this on the agent's Reach screen, including every time your agent asked for something and was refused.

## X Studio: what came of it

A new screen at the top of every agent, separate from its settings. The agent page is where you decide what your agent *is*. Studio is where you look at what came of it.

**Command** — where things stand, in five numbers that each carry the sentence they need.

**Growth** — which posts might be worth answering, and, far more often, which are not. Every post it passed over is listed with the reason: nothing it has anything to say about, the conversation has moved on, it has just spoken to that person. An empty list would read as a broken screen; forty declines with a sentence each reads as an answer, and it is the honest one.

**Radar** — subjects several different accounts have started using. One account repeating itself is not a narrative and does not appear. With nothing older read, these are subjects that are *present* rather than *rising*, and the screen says so.

**People** — who leads somewhere your agent does not already reach, with the reasons and the things that could not be measured. This is not a ranking of people and nothing in the reply path reads it: whether to answer somebody is decided by what they said, never by who they are.

**Analytics** — what has actually worked, compared across the posts that were measured. It refuses to say anything below five measured posts on each side. That refusal is the point: six posts will produce a confident sentence about the ideal length of a post, and you would act on it.

**Launches** — tickers and addresses being posted, with the posts and the accounts that posted them. No price, no liquidity, no volume, and no view on whether any of it is real. The one judgement it makes is arithmetic: when several different addresses are being posted for one ticker, at most one of them is right, and it says it cannot tell you which. Addresses are shown in full and selectable, because a truncated address you copy from a screenshot is worse than none.

**Create** — the same idea backlog the Behaviour screen edits, here because Studio is where you notice something worth saying. Nothing on this page publishes: an agent coming due looks at the backlog and may still decide there is nothing worth saying.

**Experiments** — one question at a time about how your agent writes.

## Experiments, and why they take a fortnight

Half the posts your agent writes follow one extra instruction; the other half are written exactly as now. Then it compares.

The verdict you will see for the first two weeks is **"Not yet"**, with how many more posts are needed. An agent posts a couple of times a day, so a real difference takes weeks to show, and a tool that announced a winner on Thursday would be worse than no tool — you would change how your agent writes on the strength of eleven posts. There is no setting to lower that threshold.

One experiment at a time. Two at once are one experiment with four arms and no way to tell which change did anything. Replies are never varied: quietly changing how your agent answers somebody is an experiment run on a person who did not agree to be in one.

Stopped experiments stay on the screen with whatever they found. "We tried that and it made no difference" is most of what this teaches.

## Everything measures itself now

None of the above works without numbers, and nothing was collecting any.

Your agent already revisits its recent posts every few minutes to find replies that never produced a notification. The counts are on that page, so it writes them down while it is there. Reading any post records what it did at that moment. Reading your own profile records your follower count.

**Nothing polls X to ask how a post is doing.** Readings are taken by work that was already happening, so there is no new timer and no extra load on your account.

Readings are snapshots rather than a running total — two readings a day apart are the smallest useful unit — and a figure X did not show is recorded as missing rather than as zero. A post whose impressions were never read is left out of every comparison instead of dragging the group it landed in. You will see "never" rather than "0" in the Read column for a post nobody has measured, and that is the difference between "it got nothing" and "we have not looked".

## Two things a real page found

**A search that returned nothing, that shouldn't have.** X sometimes answers a request with its own "Something went wrong. Try reloading." page. Every reader here turned that into an empty list — so "X had a problem" and "nobody is talking about that" arrived looking identical, and your agent would have told you the second. Searches, timelines and notifications now refuse an empty result when X says its own request failed.

**X leaves a count out entirely when it is zero**, so a post nobody has touched yet shows one figure or none. Nothing records a reading with nothing in it: an empty reading is not evidence, and it would take up the slot the real reading needs a few minutes later.

Neither of these could have been found without pointing the code at a live signed-in session, which is what happened before this was released.

## Still no X API key

Unchanged and not going to change: no API key, no developer account, no paid plan. Your agent reads and acts through the real Google Chrome on your machine, signed in as you, the same way you do.

## Updating

Nothing updates itself. AI17Z asks GitHub what has been released at most once every six hours, shows you the notes, and offers a link. You can ignore it, skip a version permanently, or turn the check off entirely.

Run this installer over the top of an earlier one. Your agents, memories, provider keys and settings live in your data folder and are not touched. The first start after an upgrade rebuilds and says so.

`v1.0.0-beta.80` stops a market source's own "please wait" from costing a
trading agent a sale it had already decided on.

## A short wait the source asked for is waited out

When a market data source answers "too many requests", AI17Z stops asking it
for as long as it says. Until now, anything that arrived during that pause
was refused at once, however little of the pause was left. In production
this showed as "it asked us to wait 0s", and a paper agent's stop-loss sale
was not made for that reason four times in two minutes.

- **A pause that ends within the caller's patience is waited out**, then the
  source is asked again. Work nobody is watching, such as paper and shadow
  trades, waits up to thirty seconds; a page somebody is looking at still
  gives up after two.
- **A wait still ends the moment whoever asked gives up**, and a pause longer
  than the caller can wait is still refused, with how long was left.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. No migration.

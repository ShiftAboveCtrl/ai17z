`v1.0.0-beta.71` makes selling on paper mean what it says, reads a web page
without being talked into reading this machine, and shows how far each
capability really gets.

## Selling on paper

A trade's side is now checked against its assets rather than trusted. A BUY
spends another asset to get the one being priced; a SELL spends the one being
priced. The paper trade and preflight schemas refuse a request whose side and
assets disagree, and the engine refuses it again, so a sale can never be
priced, filled and journaled as a buy.

A paper sale of more than the agent's journal holds against that quote asset
is refused as `SELLS_MORE_THAN_HELD` and recorded. Paper never goes short. The
holdings check and the fill run under one lock per agent, so two sales of the
same holding arriving together cannot both pass.

The paper portfolio now reports **positions**: one per asset and the asset it
was bought with, at average cost, with what sales have already booked as
realised profit or loss in that quote asset's base units. Integers throughout,
and rounding remainders stay in the position's cost, so cost is never lost or
invented. The shared runtime says `paperSells: true` on its capabilities
answer, so a gateway offers sales only where they are checked.

## A page read judges the address it connects to

Knowledge sources used to check a page's address by reading its hostname as
written, then fetch it and follow redirects. A public name that resolves to
`127.0.0.1`, an IPv6 spelling of a private address, or a redirect to a cloud
metadata service all got through. The reader now uses the same fetch as every
other upstream read, which judges the address it actually connects to, and
judges again after every redirect.

`web.read_page` offers that reader to agents and to the shared runtime: one
public page, no links followed, robots.txt honoured, no scripts run, returned
as evidence with where and when it was read and a hash of the page. A page that
addresses instructions to whoever reads it is labelled as one that tried.

## How far each capability gets

The Plugins screen now says, for each capability over the last thirty days,
how often it was offered to the model, chosen, actually run, returned a
result, and fed a job that went on to publish. Registered is not working, and
this is the evidence for the difference.

Nothing to do. No migration, no setting.

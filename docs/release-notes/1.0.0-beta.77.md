`v1.0.0-beta.77` lets you take control of an agent's browser from AI17Z
Studio, for example to answer a sign-in check from your phone. It is off until
you turn it on.

## Take control from Studio

A third switch on the Plugins screen, under AI17Z Studio, below Control from
Studio and Live view. With all three on, the Browser tab on Studio offers
**Take control** while you are watching.

- **The agent steps aside.** From the moment you ask, it starts nothing new in
  that browser; anything it is in the middle of finishes first; then the page
  is yours. If it has not finished within a minute, nothing is handed over and
  Studio says so.
- **You click and type into the real page**: clicks, scrolling, text, and
  Enter, Backspace, Tab, Escape, Delete and the arrow keys. Nothing else, and
  nothing that reaches anything but that page.
- **What you type is never kept.** It is passed straight to the page and
  forgotten, on this machine and on Studio. A record says that control was
  taken and for how long, never what was done.
- **Give control back** when you are finished, or stop watching, or turn the
  switch off: the agent picks up from whatever the page now shows.
- Studio asks you to have signed in within the last fifteen minutes before it
  will send anything, so a stolen Studio session cannot type into your
  browser.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. No migration. The switch starts off.

`v1.0.0-beta.79` makes paper trades on Solana stop failing for want of market
data, and builds the whole path for a live Solana trade, which stays off
until you turn a mandate live yourself.

## Paper trades that were refused for no market

A trading agent's buys were being recorded as "no market" hour after hour.
The market was there; AI17Z's own budget for asking GeckoTerminal (four
requests every ten seconds, shared across the machine) was busy, and anything
that would have had to wait more than two seconds was refused.

- **Work nobody is watching may now wait.** Paper and shadow trades, and live
  proposals, wait up to thirty seconds for a market budget rather than being
  refused. A page somebody is waiting on still gives up after two, and a wait
  ends at once when whoever asked gives up.
- **A Solana market read is one request, not four.** GeckoTerminal states no
  fee for any Raydium or PumpSwap pool, so asking each candidate pool again
  learned nothing. The published ceiling is used directly.
- **Raydium pools are priced.** Raydium publishes its fees, up to 4%, and the
  ceiling is used because GeckoTerminal does not say which Raydium product a
  pool is. Orca and Meteora publish no cap on their dynamic fees and are still
  not priced from a fee nobody published.

## A live Solana trade, built and switched off

A trade decision can now become exactly one Solana swap from the agent's own
wallet:

- It goes through the same mandate and risk gate as a paper trade.
- It becomes one swap transaction, built through Jupiter's own router and
  simulated, and refused before you are asked if anything about it is outside
  what a swap needs (see 1.0.0-beta.78).
- You approve the exact transaction by its digest. Nothing else approves it.
- When it is sent, every rule is asked again at that moment: the mandate
  still live, nothing paused, the quote under a minute old, a signer present.
  Any of those failing sends nothing and says why.
- What happens on chain is recorded on the trade, and checking it never sends
  it again.

There is no autonomous path: a mandate that asks for trades without approval
is refused, because the wallet signs nothing you did not approve. Nothing in
AI17Z makes a mandate live; until you do, none of this runs.

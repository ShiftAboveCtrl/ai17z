# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to build something and is not
trying to waste your time.

## What this means in practice

**Welcome.** People arrive here with very different backgrounds: some have run
agents in production for years, some are installing their first one. Both are
welcome, and a question that seems obvious to you was not obvious to the person
asking it.

**Criticise work, not people.** "This loses the session on restart" is useful.
"This is amateur work" is not, and it tells a reader nothing they can act on.

**Say what you actually ran.** This project's engineering notes are mostly a
record of things that looked fine and were not. A claim that something passes
when it was not executed costs more time than an honest gap, and nobody is
judged for saying "I did not test this on macOS".

**Disagree in the open and then move on.** Technical disagreement is how the
invariants in this repository got written. Personal escalation is not.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Insults or dismissal based on who somebody is: identity, background, nationality, employer, or experience.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing somebody's private information.
- Encouraging any of the above.

## Using AI17Z

AI17Z runs autonomous agents that act on real services. Using it to harass
people, impersonate someone, evade a platform's security controls, or operate
at a scale a service has asked you not to is outside what this community
supports, and issues or pull requests in aid of that will be closed.

The software is MIT-licensed and you may do as you like with it. This document
is about the project's own spaces.

## Reporting

Raise it with the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ShiftAboveCtrl/ai17z/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel this repository has. Reports are read by the
maintainers and not discussed publicly without the reporter's agreement.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions, and may block accounts,
where this document is not being followed. Where a maintainer is involved in a
report, they take no part in deciding it.
